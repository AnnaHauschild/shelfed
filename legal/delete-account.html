Delete Your Account · Developer: Anna Hauschild

This page explains how to delete your Shelfed account and the data associated with it. An account in Shelfed is optional and is only created if you sign in to use the social features.

Delete your account from inside the app

  1. Open Shelfed on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (the gear / profile area).
  3. Scroll to the very bottom of Settings to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Tap it once more to confirm ("Tap again to permanently delete"). Your account and its data are then removed.

If you can't access the app

If you are unable to sign in, email shelfed.app@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify and remove your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the following from our backend:

  • Your account and sign-in email address.
  • Your profile: username, display name and profile photo.
  • Your synced shelves (watched, wishlist and favourites).
  • Your follows and follow requests.
  • Your Stories / posts and your film matches.

What is kept

Nothing about you is kept on our backend after deletion. Any data stored only on your device (your local journal, filters and settings) is removed by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.

Retention

Account deletion takes effect immediately. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days.
